How we rate suppliers

Three priority tiers, checked before every deal.

A rating is where we start, not a guarantee. Before any contract we ask for current licences (ERC; DoF FIQC for fish; DAE phytosanitary for produce), factory certificates and proof of listing for the buyer's market.

PRIORITY A

Approved & established

On an official approval list (for example SFDA), in trade-body leadership, or a major established exporter, with products that match current buyer demand.

24
PRIORITY B

Credible, to confirm

An established exporter with a narrower range, or with details still to confirm.

28
PRIORITY C

Verify first

Found in a database only, or a very narrow range. We verify the licence before any deal.

23

What we check

  1. Licence

    Export Registration Certificate (ERC), plus a DoF FIQC plant approval for fish or DAE phytosanitary registration for fresh produce.

  2. Market listing

    The plant appears on the importing market's list: EU approved establishments, SFDA, GACC or US FDA.

  3. Certification

    HACCP is required for processors. BRCGS, ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, GLOBALG.A.P., BAP, ASC, Organic and Halal are recorded where held.

  4. Lab capability

    Residue (antibiotic or pesticide), heavy metal, aflatoxin and microbiology testing per lot, in-house or at an accredited lab.

  5. Track record

    Shipment history, claims record and references from existing buyers.

  6. Agreement

    A signed NCNDA and commission agreement before any buyer details are shared.

Trade bodies and authorities we work through

  • BFVAPEA: Bangladesh Fruits, Vegetables & Allied Products Exporters' Association, for fresh produce exporters

FAQ

Supplier questions

Who can join the GFG supplier network?

Bangladeshi exporters with a valid export licence and, for fish, a DoF FIQC-approved plant. Approvals for target markets (EU, SFDA, GACC, US FDA) and certifications such as HACCP or BRCGS raise your priority.

Do you share buyer details?

Buyer details are shared after a signed NCNDA (non-circumvention, non-disclosure agreement) and commission agreement.

Is there a fee to join?

No. GFG earns an agreed commission on completed shipments.
