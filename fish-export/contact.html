FAQ

Questions buyers ask first

What does GoodBuy Foods Global do?

GoodBuy Foods Global is a Bangladesh-based sourcing and export partner that buys on the importer's behalf. We negotiate with licensed Bangladeshi plants, enforce five QC gates on every lot, check compliance for your market and manage documents and shipment, so each container lands at a profitable price.

Who does GFG represent?

The buyer. We work to your specification, carry out QC on your behalf and release a shipment only when it meets the order you placed.

What does GFG charge?

Supplier verification is US$150 per supplier; per-shipment QC and compliance (Assure) is US$290 per container, with lab tests at cost; the end-to-end Complete service is 1.5% of FOB value with a minimum of US$450 per container. Buying agents commonly charge 5–10%.

How does GFG lower my landed cost?

By buying directly from processing plants, getting competing quotes from several qualified plants, loading containers to their payload limit, specifying glaze so you pay for fish rather than ice, and using preferential duty access for Bangladeshi origin where your market grants it.

How do you reduce supply risk?

We only use plants whose licence and market listing we have checked, qualify a backup plant for each product, use L/C or document-based payment, and require independent pre-shipment inspection before the container is sealed.

Which seafood does Bangladesh export?

Mainly frozen black tiger shrimp and giant freshwater prawn (HS 0306), frozen marine fish such as hilsa, pomfret and ribbonfish (HS 0303), chilled freshwater fish (HS 0302), live mud crab and eel (HS 0301) and dried fish (HS 0305).

How much does Bangladeshi seafood and food cost?

It depends on grade, form, packing and the approvals your market needs. Public reference points include the government minimum export prices of US$12.50/kg for hilsa and US$1.60/kg FOB for aromatic rice; our price guide lists more. We return a firm, competing-plant offer within 24 hours of your specification.

What is the minimum order?

Usually one 20-foot reefer for a single product, or one 40-foot reefer split across up to four products. Samples and trial lots can be arranged.

Which payment terms are used?

Irrevocable L/C at sight or T/T with a deposit. Terms are agreed with the exporter in the proforma invoice.

How fast do you quote?

Within 24 hours of receiving species, cut, grade, packing, quantity and destination port.
