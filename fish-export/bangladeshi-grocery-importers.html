Rules by country

Checked for your market before loading.

United KingdomPre-notification on IPAFFS for food of animal origin; UK labelling and allergen rules.
United StatesFDA Prior Notice for every shipment; the importer keeps Foreign Supplier Verification records.
CanadaThe importer needs a Safe Food for Canadians (SFCR) licence; bilingual English and French labels.
Italy and the EUFish from EU-listed plants only; EU labelling, allergen and residue rules.
MalaysiaImport permits for fish; Halal recognised by JAKIM expected for many foods.
AustraliaStrict biosecurity checks; some products need an import permit (check BICON).
Gulf countriesArabic labels with production and expiry dates; Halal where required; importer registration.

Rules change. This is a starting point, not customs advice; we confirm the requirements for each shipment.

FAQ

Bengali grocery importers: common questions

Can I order a mixed container of Bangladeshi groceries?

Yes. We combine dry food, spices, snacks, oil and sweets from several suppliers in one dry container, and frozen fish, paratha and snacks in one reefer. Each line is checked before loading.

Which countries do you ship to?

Bengali grocery importers in the UK, USA, Canada, Italy and the rest of Europe, Malaysia, Australia and the Gulf. We check the rules of each market, such as UK labelling, US FDA prior notice, Canada's SFCR import licence and Australia's biosecurity conditions.

Can you supply my own brand?

Yes. Many Bangladeshi processors pack under private label. We manage the artwork review, label compliance for your market and print approval.

Do you supply fresh vegetables and fish as well?

Yes. Fresh vegetables go weekly by air from Dhaka, and freshwater and sea fish go frozen in reefer containers.

Can we talk in Bangla?

Yes. You can write or call in Bangla, English or Arabic, including on WhatsApp.
