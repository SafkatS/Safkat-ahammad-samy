Importing food and seafood from Bangladesh to the United States

The approvals, documents and checks your shipment needs, and how we make sure they are in place before loading.

In short: The plant must be registered with the FDA and follow seafood HACCP. Sea freight takes 35–45 days to New York, Savannah or Los Angeles.

Approvals and rules

  • The plant must be registered with the FDA and follow seafood HACCP.
  • Each shipment needs FDA Prior Notice.
  • Shrimp is covered by the Seafood Import Monitoring Program (SIMP).
  • The FDA Food Traceability Rule (FSMA 204) applies from 20 July 2028 and covers most seafood.

Duty and preferences

Duty depends on the HS code and current US trade measures, which have changed often. Confirm before you contract.

Rules and tariffs change. This guide is a starting point, not legal or customs advice. Updated 2026-11-14.

Documents

  • FDA Prior Notice confirmation
  • Seafood HACCP records from the processor
  • SIMP harvest and chain-of-custody data for shrimp
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading
  • Importer's Foreign Supplier Verification Program records (non-seafood foods)

Common reasons shipments are held or rejected

  • FDA import alerts for residues or filth
  • Incomplete SIMP data
  • Labelling and allergen declarations
