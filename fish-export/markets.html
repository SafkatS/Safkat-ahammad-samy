FAQ

Shipping questions

How long does a reefer take from Chattogram to Europe?

Typically 28–35 days to Rotterdam, Antwerp or Hamburg via a transshipment hub such as Colombo or Singapore.

Which Incoterms do you quote?

FOB Chattogram, CFR or CIF to your port. Air freight for live crab, eel, chilled fish and fresh produce is quoted CPT or CIP to the destination airport.

How many cartons fit in a 40-foot reefer?

About 1,900–2,100 master cartons of 10 kg frozen shrimp, or roughly 19–21 tonnes net, depending on carton size.
