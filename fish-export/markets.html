Ordering

From first message to bill of lading.

A first container usually ships 3–5 weeks after the signed contract, depending on season and grade.

  1. Request a quote

    Send species, cut, grade, pack and port. You get a price offer within 24 hours.

  2. Samples

    Frozen samples by courier on dry ice, or a video inspection of current stock.

  3. Contract & payment

    Proforma invoice. Payment by irrevocable L/C at sight or T/T with deposit.

  4. Production & inspection

    Packed to your spec. Third-party pre-shipment inspection with photos per lot.

  5. Shipment

    Reefer booked, sealed at plant, telemetry shared. Documents couriered or via eBL.

  6. Arrival & claims

    Our team supports border inspection. Quality claims answered within 48 hours.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask first

What is your minimum order?

One 20′ reefer for a single product, or one 40′ reefer that can be split across up to four products. Retail private label starts at one 40′ container.

Can you export hilsa?

Hilsa export from Bangladesh needs a government permit and is limited to published quotas and seasons. Tell us your volume and we will confirm availability under the current quota.

Which payment terms do you accept?

Irrevocable L/C at sight from a first-class bank, or T/T with 30 % advance and the balance against scanned documents. Repeat buyers can apply for CAD terms.

Do you pack under our brand?

Yes. We print retail bags and master cartons to your artwork, including EAN/UPC barcodes, multilingual labels and the EU establishment number.

How do you handle quality claims?

Send photos and the lot code within 7 days of arrival. We respond within 48 hours and settle agreed claims by credit note or replacement on the next shipment.
