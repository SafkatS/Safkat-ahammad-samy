FAQ

Service questions

Who is responsible for quality checking?

We are. Under Assure and Complete, our QC officers or an independent inspector check every gate against your specification, and a lot that fails does not ship. You approve the evidence before you pay the balance.

What happens if the goods do not match the specification on arrival?

Under Complete, we handle the claim with the exporter within 48 hours and refund our full service fee for that shipment. Under Assure, we handle the claim with you and provide all inspection evidence.

Do I still pay the exporter directly?

Yes. You pay the exporter by L/C or T/T against documents, so your payment is protected by the banking process. Our service fee is invoiced separately.

Can I use only the verification service?

Yes. Verify is a one-time check of a supplier you have found yourself, with a written report in 5 working days.
