Who we serve best

Three kinds of buyer.

We focus where Bangladesh is strongest and where a buyer-side partner makes the biggest difference.

Seafood importers in the EU, UK and Gulf

Full containers of shrimp, prawn and marine fish.

Plants listed for your market, count and glaze control, programmes for monthly volume.

Typical lines: Black tiger shrimp, Giant freshwater prawn, Silver pomfret, Ribbonfish

South Asian grocery wholesalers and distributors

Mixed containers: freshwater fish, dried fish, rice, spices, snacks and frozen foods.

One order, one QC standard and one set of documents across many small suppliers.

Typical lines: Rohu, Pabda catfish, Dried Bombay duck, Aromatic rice (Kalijira, Chinigura, Kataribhog), Puffed rice, Frozen paratha

Food service and private-label buyers

Specification-driven lines with your own packing and label.

Label compliance review, pack approval and the same specification on every shipment.

Typical lines: Value-added shrimp, Pangasius, Frozen vegetables, Mango pulp & purée

FAQ

Common questions

Who pays GoodBuy Foods Global?

The buyer. We charge buyers a published service fee and take no commission from suppliers, so our supplier ratings and QC decisions stay independent.

Can small importers use GFG?

Yes. We consolidate mixed containers across several suppliers, so a wholesaler can order many products in one shipment.

What does a supplier gain if GFG takes no commission?

Verified buyers with real specifications, independent QC evidence that supports a fair price, and repeat orders through buyer supply programmes.
