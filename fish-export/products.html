63 seafood and 40 food products exported from Bangladesh, with scientific names, Bangla names, HS headings, product forms and main buyer markets.

In short: Bangladesh's main seafood exports are frozen shrimp and prawn (HS 0306), frozen marine fish (0303), chilled freshwater fish (0302), live crab and eel (0301) and dried fish (0305). Food exports include aromatic rice, spices, fresh vegetables, mango, frozen snacks and packaged foods.
