Importing food and seafood from Bangladesh to Qatar

The approvals, documents and checks your shipment needs, and how we make sure they are in place before loading.

In short: Food imports are checked by the Ministry of Public Health before release. Sea freight takes 16–22 days to Hamad Port, usually via Jebel Ali or Colombo.

Approvals and rules

  • Food imports are checked by the Ministry of Public Health before release.
  • GCC (GSO) standards apply, including Arabic labelling with production and expiry dates.
  • Halal certification is required for products of animal origin and requested by most buyers for processed food.

Duty and preferences

Duty depends on the HS code under the GCC common tariff. Confirm with your customs broker.

Rules and tariffs change. This guide is a starting point, not legal or customs advice. Updated 2026-11-24.

Documents

  • Health or phytosanitary certificate
  • Halal certificate where required
  • Certificate of origin
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading

Common reasons shipments are held or rejected

  • Arabic label missing or incomplete
  • Temperature abuse for chilled produce
  • Missing Halal certificate
